Kion released version 3.16 with direct integration for tracking Anthropic AI token spending alongside cloud costs, enabling unified budget management and governance ahead of the FinOps X conference. The platform introduces Automation Policies for common cloud hygiene tasks such as auto-tagging resources and scheduling instance shutdowns, plus SKU Meter reporting for granular cost visibility. These enhancements help organizations bridge the insights-to-action gap by helping cloud and FinOps teams act on cloud and AI token spend insights more efficiently with automation.
